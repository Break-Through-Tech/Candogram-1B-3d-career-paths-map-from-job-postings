# Data and modeling plan

- **Prepare job profiles.** Keep one job per `Job ID`. Preserve title, agency, category, career level, description, qualifications, and optional preferred skills. Check duplicate postings, missing fields, repeated text, and misleading seniority labels. Do not treat a job's requirements as skills its current holder necessarily has.
- **Define the review standard.** Have people assess varied job-to-job moves and complete routes: plausible next step, aspirational goal, implausible move, and insufficient evidence. Include free-text goals outside the dataset and open-ended requests. Record reasons; the postings contain no observed career histories.
- **Connect profiles and requests to the job graph.** Represent a user's stated current role, skills, experience, education, and licenses alongside the jobs. Resolve a selected posting, job search, or natural-language request into a specific job, a target role, or an open-ended search for promising careers. Ask for clarification when a request is ambiguous; do not invent qualifications. Profile embeddings and job matching support retrieval, not proof of eligibility.
- **Build directed edges and goal-directed routes.** Use duties, skills, seniority, and qualifications to propose and score moves between jobs. Add the user as the starting point, then rank direct next steps and distinct aspirational routes toward a target; for open-ended requests, rank reachable destinations. Show which requirements the profile meets, lacks, or has not confirmed. An intermediate job does not automatically grant its required skills. Skip a separate title-only prototype.
- **Review and refine recommendations.** Compare routes with the human-reviewed examples. Check false promotions, licensing and degree requirements, unrelated occupations, repeated results, unsupported skill acquisition, and misleading confidence. Adjust edge and route rules based on failures; do not claim observed transition probabilities.
- **Prepare the 3D model output.** Give each job stable identifiers, 3D display coordinates, career level, and links to suggested paths and their evidence. Use dimensionality reduction for the map only; rank transitions and construct paths from the richer job representation, never from 3D distance.

Research basis: [Sentence Transformers on short-query versus long-document search](https://www.sbert.net/examples/sentence_transformer/applications/semantic-search/README.html) and [retrieve-then-rerank](https://www.sbert.net/examples/cross_encoder/applications/README.html). These are candidate techniques, not committed model choices; select them after reviewing results on this dataset.

# 05 — Explore career directions without a goal

**What to build:** A user supplies a profile without a destination. The model shows relevant jobs now and varied possible future directions with short reasons.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Recommend jobs from a profile; 03 — Score a directed job move.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The results distinguish immediately relevant jobs from future or adjacent directions.
- [ ] Suggestions span meaningful roles rather than repeating near-identical titles or agencies.
- [ ] Future directions use the same move policy as goal-directed routing and show important profile gaps without assuming skills were acquired.
